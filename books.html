Books

Last updated Apr 16, 2026

A working reading list. Ordered by tier, not by how much I recommend any single book. Later tiers assume you've worked through the earlier ones.

Tier 1: Foundations of Civilization

  • Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind, Yuval Noah Harari
  • The Dawn of Everything, David Graeber and David Wengrow
  • Debt: The First 5,000 Years, David Graeber
  • The Horse, the Wheel, and Language, David W. Anthony
  • The First Signs, Genevieve von Petzinger

Tier 2: Ancient Thought (Power and Human Nature)

  • Republic, Plato
  • History of the Peloponnesian War, Thucydides
  • Histories, Herodotus
  • The Prince, Niccolò Machiavelli
  • Leviathan, Thomas Hobbes
  • Discourse on the Origin of Inequality, Jean-Jacques Rousseau
  • The Social Contract, Jean-Jacques Rousseau
  • Meditations on First Philosophy, René Descartes

Tier 3: Religion and Meaning

  • The Elementary Forms of Religious Life, Émile Durkheim
  • Popol Vuh, K'iche' Maya (anonymous)
  • Gospel of Thomas, Nag Hammadi corpus (anonymous)
  • The Book of Enoch, pseudepigraphal (anonymous)
  • City of God, Augustine of Hippo

Tier 4: Modern Systems (Capitalism and Control)

  • The Protestant Ethic and the Spirit of Capitalism, Max Weber
  • The Philosophy of Money, Georg Simmel
  • Capital in the Twenty-First Century, Thomas Piketty
  • Seeing Like a State, James C. Scott
  • The Structure of Scientific Revolutions, Thomas S. Kuhn
  • The Innovator's Dilemma, Clayton M. Christensen
  • Chip War, Chris Miller

Tier 5: Power, Conflict, Collapse

  • On War, Carl von Clausewitz
  • Finite and Infinite Games, James P. Carse
  • The Origins of Totalitarianism, Hannah Arendt
  • The Open Society and Its Enemies, Karl Popper
  • The State and Revolution, V.I. Lenin
  • War and Peace and War, Peter Turchin
  • End Times, Peter Turchin

Tier 6: Incentives, Psychology, Prediction

  • Thinking, Fast and Slow, Daniel Kahneman
  • The Elephant in the Brain, Robin Hanson and Kevin Simler
  • Superforecasting: The Art and Science of Prediction, Philip E. Tetlock and Dan Gardner

Tier 7: Intelligence, Strategy, Application

  • The Craft of Intelligence, Allen Dulles
  • Intelligence in War, John Keegan
  • Good Strategy / Bad Strategy, Richard Rumelt

Tier 8: Execution, Performance, Output

Read early, not late.

  • Peak: Secrets from the New Science of Expertise, Anders Ericsson and Robert Pool
  • The Lean Startup, Eric Ries
  • Atomic Habits, James Clear
  • Show Your Work!, Austin Kleon

Tier 9: Elite Power and Institutions

  • The Power Elite, C. Wright Mills
  • The Anglo-American Establishment, Carroll Quigley
  • The Chosen, Jerome Karabel
  • Excellent Sheep, William Deresiewicz

Tier 10: Literature (Human Psychology)

  • Crime and Punishment, Fyodor Dostoevsky
  • The Brothers Karamazov, Fyodor Dostoevsky
  • Anna Karenina, Leo Tolstoy
  • Don Quixote, Miguel de Cervantes
  • Hamlet, William Shakespeare

Tier 11: Epics and Civilizational Myth

  • Iliad, Homer
  • Odyssey, Homer
  • Aeneid, Virgil
  • The Divine Comedy, Dante Alighieri
  • Paradise Lost, John Milton

Tier 12: Speculative / High-Risk

Last only. Read after the foundations are in place.

  • The Sovereign Individual, James Dale Davidson and William Rees-Mogg
  • Tragedy and Hope, Carroll Quigley
  • Tower of Basel, Adam LeBor
  • Confessions of an Economic Hit Man, John Perkins
  • The Secret History of the World, Mark Booth
  • Foundations of Geopolitics, Aleksandr Dugin
  • The Alignment Problem, Brian Christian
